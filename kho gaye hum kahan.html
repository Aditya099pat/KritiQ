Storyline

Mumbai, the present. Ahana, Imaad, and Neil — three best friends from college — are navigating their mid-twenties with all the chaos and confusion that entails. Ahana is a fitness influencer who filters her real life through a perfectly curated Instagram feed, hiding her loneliness behind likes and followers. Imaad is a DJ still carrying a heartbreak he can't admit to, using Tinder and music to fill the void. Neil, the most grounded of the three, finds himself in a relationship with a woman who exists only as a social media presence to his friends.

Kho Gaye Hum Kahan is one of the most honest films about how social media shapes and distorts our real relationships, identities, and sense of self. Director Arjun Varain Singh crafts a warm but unflinching portrait of a generation that is hyperconnected and yet profoundly lonely. The three central performances are beautifully nuanced, and the film's love for Mumbai comes through in every frame.
