Storyline

The Boys is set in a world where superheroes are celebrated celebrities, worshipped by the public and owned by Vought International — a mega-corporation hiding their corruption behind polished PR. When Hughie Campbell's girlfriend is brutally killed by a careless speedster, he's dragged into a covert team of vigilantes led by the ruthless Billy Butcher, a man with a deep personal vendetta against Homelander, the world's most powerful — and most dangerous — superhero.

Together, The Boys wage a guerrilla war against the Seven, exposing the truth behind the capes. The show is a biting satire on celebrity culture, corporate greed, media manipulation, and the cult of power. Dark, brutal, and wickedly funny — it asks what happens when those meant to protect us are the ones we need protecting from.
