Storyline

Peter Parker is a gifted but lonely teenager raised by his Aunt May and Uncle Ben, still haunted by the mysterious disappearance of his parents years ago. When a chance visit to Oscorp leads to a bite from a genetically engineered spider, Peter finds himself transformed — wall-crawling, super-strong, and suddenly capable of creating web-shooters of his own design.

But with newfound power comes a lesson in responsibility, learned the hardest possible way. As he crafts the identity of Spider-Man, Peter must also battle the monstrous Lizard — the alter ego of his one-time mentor Dr. Curt Connors — and navigate his feelings for Gwen Stacy. Marc Webb's reboot brings a more grounded, romantic tone to the Spider-Man mythology, with Andrew Garfield delivering a charming, emotionally rich performance.
