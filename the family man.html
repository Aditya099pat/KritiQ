Storyline

Srikant Tiwari is a mid-level officer at TASC — a fictional counter-terrorism agency under the Indian government — and he is absolutely terrible at being a family man. He forgets anniversaries, misses school events, and his wife Suchi is seriously considering divorce. But when he's not being an absent husband and father, he's dismantling terrorist plots that could kill thousands.

Season 1 pits Srikant against a devastating attack planned deep within a militant network, while Season 2 — widely considered one of the finest seasons of Indian television — introduces Raji, a Sri Lankan Tamil militant played by a mesmerising Samantha Ruth Prabhu, as the primary threat. The Family Man perfectly blends genuine espionage tension with domestic comedy and surprising emotional depth. Manoj Bajpayee is simply one of the best actors working anywhere in the world.
