Storyline

Kabir Singh is Delhi's most brilliant and most volatile surgeon. At the top of his medical college, feared and admired in equal measure, he is also a man of unchecked rage and reckless abandon. When he falls fiercely in love with Preeti, a quiet first-year student, their bond is intense and all-consuming.

But when Preeti's family forces her into an arranged marriage, Kabir spirals — into alcohol, drugs, and self-destruction on a level that threatens to consume everything he has built. The film, a remake of the Telugu hit Arjun Reddy, is a raw, divisive portrait of obsessive love and self-destruction. Shahid Kapoor delivers a career-defining performance, and Kiara Advani brings quiet strength to Preeti. An enormous box office success, it sparked as much debate as it did acclaim.
