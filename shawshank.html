Storyline

The Shawshank Redemption is the story of Andy Dufresne — a banker wrongfully convicted of his wife's murder — who is sentenced to life at Shawshank State Penitentiary. What follows is two decades of brutality, corruption, and despair, yet Andy never surrenders his spirit. Through patience, intelligence and an unbreakable inner hope, he befriends Ellis "Red" Redding and quietly transforms the lives of those around him.

More than a prison film, it's a meditation on freedom, dignity, and the indomitable human will. Frank Darabont's masterpiece, adapted from Stephen King's novella, remains the #1 rated film on IMDB — a testament to its universal, timeless resonance. "Get busy living, or get busy dying."
