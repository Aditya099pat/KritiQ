Two political prisoners plan a high-risk escape from Pretoria Central Prison during apartheid-era South Africa. The film focuses on intelligence, courage, and the tension of a near-impossible breakout.

🔐 Prison Escape Thriller — tense, intelligent and compact
Overview

Escape from Pretoria delivers a focused survival-thriller experience built around careful planning, pressure, and suspense. It is a strong fit for viewers who enjoy prison-break stories with realistic stakes rather than exaggerated action.

KritiQ Review

The film works best when it slows down and lets the tension build through small details: keys, routines, timing, and silence. Daniel Radcliffe gives the story a grounded center, while the direction keeps the escape mechanics clear and engaging.
