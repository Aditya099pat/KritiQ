Storyline

Agneepath — meaning "the path of fire" — is a story of a son's burning vow of revenge. Young Vijay Deenanath Chauhan watches in horror as his beloved father, a respected village teacher, is falsely accused and brutally lynched by the drug lord Kancha Cheena, who then seizes control of their village, Mandwa.

Forced to flee with his mother and newborn sister to the slums of Mumbai, Vijay grows up with one consuming purpose: to return to Mandwa and destroy Kancha. He rises through the city's criminal underworld with ruthless intelligence, carving a path through gang lords and corrupt officials — all while trying to protect his family and keep alive his father's legacy of dignity. Hrithik Roshan delivers the performance of his career, and Sanjay Dutt is absolutely terrifying as Kancha Cheena — one of Bollywood's greatest villains.
