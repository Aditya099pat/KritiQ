Storyline

Paul Atreides is the son of a noble family, a gifted young man who can see fragments of the future in his dreams. When House Atreides is given stewardship of Arrakis — the harsh desert planet that produces the universe's most precious substance, the spice Melange — it seems like an opportunity. It is a trap.

After betrayal destroys his family and casts him into the merciless desert, Paul must find his place among the Fremen — the fierce, blue-eyed natives who have survived Arrakis for generations. Guided by visions and the bond he forms with Chani, Paul begins to discover that his destiny may be larger — and more dangerous — than anything he imagined. Denis Villeneuve's adaptation of Frank Herbert's impossible novel is a triumph of scale and atmosphere.
