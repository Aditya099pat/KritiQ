Storyline

Farhan and Raju are two friends searching for their long-lost college buddy Rancho — the most unconventional, free-spirited engineering student the Imperial College of Engineering ever saw. As they travel to find him, the film unspools their years together under the tyrannical "Virus" — the institute's rule-obsessed director — and how Rancho's philosophy of learning through passion rather than fear changed everything.

3 Idiots is simultaneously a riotous comedy, a sharp critique of India's exam-obsessed education system, and a deeply moving friendship story. Aamir Khan's Rancho is one of Hindi cinema's most beloved characters, and Rajkumar Hirani's direction ensures that every laugh has a heart and every message lands without feeling preachy. A film that generations of Indian students have claimed as their own.
