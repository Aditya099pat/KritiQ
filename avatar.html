Storyline

On the lush, bioluminescent moon of Pandora, former Marine Jake Sully is given a second chance. Paralyzed from the waist down, he remotely inhabits an Avatar — a genetically engineered Na'vi body — to infiltrate the indigenous people on behalf of a mining corporation hungry for a rare mineral called Unobtanium.

But as Jake immerses himself in Na'vi culture, learns their language, and falls for the fearless warrior Neytiri, he finds himself torn between the world he came from and the world he's falling in love with. When the corporation launches a devastating attack, Jake must choose a side — and become the unlikely hero of an alien world. James Cameron's revolutionary visual epic changed the language of cinema with its groundbreaking use of 3D and motion capture technology.
