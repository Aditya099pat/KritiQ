Storyline

In the Seven Kingdoms of Westeros, nine noble houses wage war for the Iron Throne — a seat of power that corrupts all who seek it. The Starks of the North cling to honor in a world where honor gets you killed. The Lannisters rule with gold and ruthlessness. Across the Narrow Sea, the exiled Daenerys Targaryen rises from slave markets to command armies and dragons.

Meanwhile, beyond the great Wall at the edge of the world, an ancient supernatural threat known as the White Walkers stirs for the first time in thousands of years. Game of Thrones is a story of shifting alliances, brutal power, shattered innocence, and the fundamental question: what does it truly cost to sit on the throne? A landmark of prestige television that redefined what was possible on screen.
